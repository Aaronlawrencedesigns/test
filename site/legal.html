Terms of Service

What you're buying

For a one-time payment, we make a custom animated version of your pet (a “Sidekick”) from the photos you upload, and give you a personal pet code to use it in the Desktop Sidekicks app for Mac. Your Sidekick is generated with AI image and video tools and checked by us before it's delivered. It's a likeness of your pet, not a recording: small differences in markings, proportions or movement are part of how the technology works.

Your photos

  • Only upload photos you took or have the right to use, of a pet that's yours or that you have permission to feature.
  • Don't upload photos of people, or anything illegal, hateful or explicit. We may refuse or cancel an order (with a full refund) if photos aren't suitable.
  • You keep ownership of your photos. You give us permission to use them only to make, check, deliver and support your Sidekick.

Using your Sidekick

  • Your Sidekick is for your personal, non-commercial use, on any Macs you own. Keep your pet code private: anyone with it can download your pet.
  • Don't resell, redistribute or reverse-engineer the app or the pet files, or use them to build a competing service.
  • The Desktop Sidekicks app, its design and our software stay ours. The animations of your pet are made for you, and we won't sell or publish them.

Delivery

We usually deliver within a day of payment, by email to the address you gave at checkout. Sometimes it takes longer, for example when we remake parts of a pet to get them right. If we can't make a Sidekick that meets our standards from your photos, we'll ask for new photos or refund you in full.

Requirements

The app runs on macOS 12 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. A Windows version isn't available yet.

No warranty, limits

The service and app are provided “as is”. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect or consequential losses, and our total liability for any claim is limited to the amount you paid for the order in question. Nothing here limits rights you have under consumer law that can't be waived.

Changes

We may update these terms. The version that applies to your order is the one shown when you paid. If we make significant changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Refund Policy

We want you to love your Sidekick. Because each one is made specifically for your pet, here's how refunds work:

  • Before we start: if you change your mind before we begin making your pet, email us and we'll refund you in full.
  • If it doesn't look right: if your Sidekick doesn't look like your pet, email us within 14 days of delivery with what's wrong. We'll take a look and, if it isn't right, refund you in full.
  • If it doesn't work: if the app won't run on your Mac (macOS 12 or later) and we can't fix it, we'll refund you in full.
  • If we can't make it: if your photos can't produce a good Sidekick and you don't want to send new ones, we'll refund you in full.

We can't offer refunds just for a change of mind after your Sidekick has been delivered and works as described. Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

To request a refund: email hello@desktopsidekicks.com from the address you ordered with, and include your pet's name. Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; we're happy to help.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Order details: your email address, your pet's name, species, sex and whether it has a tail, and the photos you upload.
  • Payment: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number; we receive a confirmation that you paid, the amount, and your email.
  • Pet files: the animations and images we make of your pet, and a quality report.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell your information.

How we use it

  • To make, check and deliver your Sidekick, and to fix it if needed.
  • To email you about your order (confirmation, delivery, support replies). We don't send marketing emails unless you ask for them.
  • To handle refunds, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers, only for the purposes above:

  • Stripe: payments.
  • Cloudflare: website hosting and private storage for your photos and pet files.
  • Postmark: order emails.
  • AI image and video generation providers: your photos are sent to them to generate your pet's images and animations.
  • DigitalOcean: the server that makes your pet.

The Mac app

The Desktop Sidekicks app downloads your pet using your pet code. It follows your mouse pointer on your own computer only: pointer positions, your screen and your activity are never sent to us. The app doesn't include analytics or tracking.

How long we keep it

We keep your photos and pet files while you might need them: to let you download your pet again and to fix it if something's wrong. Email us at any time and we'll delete your photos and pet files (your pet code will then stop working). We keep payment and order records as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or tell us how it's used, by emailing hello@desktopsidekicks.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have additional rights, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our service isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Security

Photos and pet files are stored privately and only shared through short-lived links or your personal pet code. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.
