About

A sidekick that looks exactly like yours

Desktop Sidekicks started with Chunky, a lilac merle French Bulldog with pale blue eyes. We wanted him on the desk all day, so we built a small Mac app where he sits in the corner, watches the cursor and does his tricks. People kept asking for their own, so now anyone can have one.

Your pet isn't a cartoon or a 3D model. Every frame is photo-real footage generated from your photos, then checked by a quality pipeline before it's sent to you.

FAQ

Questions

Does it work on Windows?

Not yet. Sidekicks run on macOS today. Windows is next.

What photos work best?

Four clear, well-lit photos of the same pet: from the front, from the side, from behind and sitting. The whole pet in frame, no other animals or people.

Will it slow down my Mac?

No. Your pet is a small app that plays short clips and shows one still frame at a time while it follows the mouse.

Does it get in the way?

Clicks pass straight through everywhere except your pet itself. You can resize it, move it, or put it to sleep from the menu.

What happens to my photos?

We use them only to make your pet. They're never shared or shown publicly.

How long does it take?

Usually within a day. We email you as soon as your pet passes its checks.
